## Frame 02 — La Tormenta

```
A massive sandstorm engulfing the Atacama desert flatlands. A towering wall of sand hundreds of meters high advances across the plain, consuming everything. The sky transitions from clear amber to a terrifying dark brown wall. A few tiny human silhouettes are barely visible at the base of the storm, being swallowed. Total chaos at the storm's edge — visibility dropping to near zero. The scale of the natural force is overwhelming and absolute. Cinematic wide shot, warm amber tones bleeding into dark oppressive brown, dramatic and terrifying.
```

---

## Frame 03 — La Cueva / Entrada

```
A narrow crack in a red sandstone desert hillside, the entrance to an unexpected cave. Contraluz composition — the dark interior of the cave opening contrasts against the still hazy amber desert light outside. The opening is just wide enough for a single person to enter sideways. Ancient eroded rock on both sides. The threshold between the known desert world and the unknown underground. Cinematic, atmospheric, deep shadow inside vs warm desert glow outside, a sense of crossing a boundary.
```

---

## Frame 04 — Los Túneles / Las Inscripciones

```
Dark underground tunnels carved in ancient rock, stretching deep into the distance. The walls are completely covered in mysterious inscriptions in an unknown language, emitting a faint blue-green bioluminescent glow — the only light source in total darkness. The tunnel vanishes into perspective depth. The glowing inscriptions feel ancient, deliberate, patient. No human figures. Only rock, absolute darkness, and the quiet glow of writing that has waited centuries. Cinematic underground atmosphere, teal and green accent light against deep blacks and dark stone.
```

---

## Frame 05 — La Primera Caverna / La Bruja

```
A vast cathedral-like underground cavern. Every surface of the walls and ceiling is covered in glowing inscriptions. At the center of the cavern floor, a single small solitary figure stands perfectly still — waiting, calm, as if this moment was always going to happen. The cavern ceiling is lost in darkness far above. The scale of the space completely dominates the lone figure. Faint warm amber-gold light from the inscriptions illuminates the space. Eerie symmetry and stillness. Cinematic wide angle, the figure small against the immensity of the space.
```

---

## Frame 06 — La Visión / Memoria del Desierto

```
A ghostly double exposure: the underground cave walls become semi-transparent, and bleeding through them is a vision of the salitrera at its peak — workers in period clothing, smoke rising from chimneys, the town full of life and movement. Two realities overlap — the silent present cave and the vivid remembered past. Spectral and melancholic. Warm sepia tones of the memory bleed into the cold blue of the cave. The past refuses to fully disappear. Cinematic double exposure, layered transparencies, the living memory of a place that suffered.
```

---

## Frame 07 — La Cámara del Río de Arena

```
A vast underground chamber where physics is broken. A river of sand flows horizontally at high speed through the middle of the cavern — completely defying gravity, flowing sideways like a river but made of golden desert sand. Small isolated rock platforms float throughout the space. The sand river catches faint light and glows slightly where disturbed. The ceiling is far above, lost in darkness. The impossible rendered mundane and normalized. Cinematic and surreal, otherworldly beauty, warm golden sand against cool dark stone.
```

---

## Frame 08 — Los Túneles de los Duendes

```
Narrow oppressive rock tunnels in deep darkness. Extreme chiaroscuro — almost everything shadow. Small humanoid figures lurk in cracks and crevices of the rock walls — Chilean folklore duendes, not fantasy goblins but something distinctly local and unsettling, small and mischievous. Their eyes catch pinpoints of light. The walls feel alive with hidden watchers. The tunnel itself seems to breathe. Cinematic folklore horror, playful but genuinely threatening, deep shadow with only the faintest light catching eyes and edges.
```

---

## Frame 09 — La Cámara Final / El Talismán

```
An enormous ancient underground chamber, the largest yet. At its center, five glowing stone fragments float in a deliberate formation, connected by visible streams of flowing sand and golden light — like energy veins between them. From this formation, a massive whirlwind spirals upward and exits through multiple carved tunnel openings in the chamber walls. The talisman radiates warm golden-amber light that fills the entire cavern, illuminating ancient carved walls. Cinematic high angle looking down, ancient power made visible, beautiful and ominous.
```

---

## Frame 10 — El Techo / La Bestia

```
Worm's eye view looking directly upward at the cave ceiling. Almost completely camouflaged within the stone — indistinguishable from the rock itself — an enormous creature: half minotaur, half guanaco (South American camelid with long neck and soft face). Its stone-textured body merges with the ceiling. It is colossal — its body fills the entire frame. The talisman's warm glow from below catches its outline, revealing it. The duality of the terrifying and the absurd — ancient monster fused with a guanaco. Cinematic, dramatic worm's eye, shadows and golden light from below.
```

## Frame 11 (variante de perspectiva) — La Salida

```
View from inside a cave looking outward through the cave mouth. The opening frames the outside world like a painting. Silhouettes of small figures stand at the threshold looking out at their first view of Santa Añañuca — the salitrera town in a valley, surrounded by mountains, with a field of Añañuca flowers in impossible shifting colors. The contrast between the dark cave interior and the vibrant illuminated world outside. The end of one journey and the beginning of another. Cinematic, hopeful and weighted simultaneously.
```
